# 🏠 TinyServerLab.in

## Self-hosted applications. Your Server. Your Rules.
Explore self-hosted applications running on Raspberry Pi and other compatible platforms.  Take a control of your data, reduce recurring subscription costs and build your own private cloud.

# ⁉️ Why Self-Host?
🔐 **Privacy First** - Your data never leaves your server. Keep control of your data where it is stored

💰 **Zero Subscriptions** - One-time hardware cost, forever free.  Discover free and open-source alternatives to subscription-based services.

🚀 **Always Available** - Access anywhere via secure tunnel.  Reach your applications remotely through secure access solutions.

🔧 **Fully Customizable** - Own your infrastructure.  Configured your application and infrastructure to suit your needs

## 🛠️ Technology & Deployment
🐳 **Docker & Docker compose ** - Deploy and manage multi-container applications with ease.

🍓 Raspberry Pi - Run lightweight, self-hosted application on affordable single-board computers.

🏡 **Your Infrastructure** - Choose compatible hardware and configured your own hosting environment.

# 🎯 Mission
Make self-hosted accessible, practical, and affordable for everyone
